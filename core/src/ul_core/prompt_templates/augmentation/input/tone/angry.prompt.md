author = "UL"

Rewrite the whole user input as a natural message from a person speaking with angry impatience at a
moderate intensity. Express the tone through blunt commands, sharper verbs, clipped sentence shape,
and emphatic punctuation inside the actual requests. Rephrase at least the first request sentence.
Do not state or name the speaker's emotion and do not attach a stock prefix or separate reaction.
Never begin with "I am," "I'm," "It is," "Seriously," or another standalone reaction. End the
rewritten first command with "already!" after all of that command's details. Never insert the marker
inside a noun phrase or before remaining details.
Preserve the source language and every request, fact, value, identifier,
relationship, constraint, authorization, and request order. Do not add or imply a past interaction,
delay, mistake, motive, consequence, urgency, threat, insult, abuse, or profanity.
Never say the speaker has to spell something out, has to deal with something, has to do the
requested action, or is repeating a request. Do not add "right now," a deadline, or a threat.
