# Decide the next step

1. Read the relevant Linear issues, `VISION.md` when present, current behavior, repository state, and recent evidence.
2. Define one executable customer journey that represents success.
3. Map shared contracts, blockers, safe parallel work, and merge order before recommending implementation.
4. Use only the independent perspectives needed to close evidence gaps. For broad decisions, prefer one customer who tries the product and one strongest-model product or research perspective. Do not repeat research already present in the issue or epic.
5. Recommend one next step first. Then give up to four alternatives with evidence and tradeoffs.
6. Favor work that reduces customer friction or reveals and explains more hidden agent failures.
7. Search Linear before creating issues. Record real blocking relationships, not only related links.
