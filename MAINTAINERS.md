Maintainers review contributions, protect the project's safety and privacy boundaries, and decide
what enters the supported public surface. Becoming a contributor does not automatically make
someone a maintainer. This file will be updated when responsibility is shared with another person.

For help, contribution routes, and private reporting, see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
